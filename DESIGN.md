## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, boutique software marketplace aesthetic engineered for high transactional velocity and verified trust. The brand balances Silicon Valley fintech precision with hyper-localized regional commerce mechanisms. 

### Visual Movement
The interface executes an **Elevated Corporate Minimalist** style: clean structural layouts, controlled contrast, and luminous surface layering. It repudiates cluttered classifieds in favor of razor-sharp software cataloging. Clean micro-borders delineate high-value digital deliverables, while targeted jewel-toned accents drive transactional triggers.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Audience:** Developers, digital agency founders, e-commerce entrepreneurs, and independent creators seeking pre-built web solutions, script licenses, and turnkey digital businesses.
- **Emotional Response:** Instantaneous transactional confidence, enterprise-grade engineering credibility, premium exclusivity, and immediate clarity regarding product safety and verification.

## Colors

The palette leverages a deep enterprise teal anchor for institutional trust, offset by warm transaction cues and slate structural tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F766E`):** Represents growth, security, and verified authenticity. Applied to core call-to-action buttons, verified status elements, key active indicators, and high-impact badges.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`):** An energetic warm amber that powers product ratings, featured project badges, transactional discount highlights, and instant checkout triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`):** Technical clarity blue, allocated to architecture tags, license tier badges, framework filters (e.g., Next.js, Laravel), and documentation links.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0F172A`):** Deep charcoal slate providing stark, readable contrast for high-order typography and data tables.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, low-strain cool background canvas against which pure white container surfaces float.
- **Neutral Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for asset cards, technical spec drawers, and modal interactions.
- **Border Subtle (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise dividing lines for layout containment.

### Functional States & Trust Highlights
- **Success (`#059669`):** Download availability, transaction complete, license validated.
- **Warning (`#D97706`):** Limited stock for single-sale website transfers, expiring promotion.
- **Danger (`#DC2626`):** Failed transaction, invalid license domain.
- **MFS Payment Accents:** Dedicated sub-tokens harmonize local payment rails (bKash pink-crimson `#E2136E` and Nagad orange-red `#F7941D`) through muted secondary badges without destabilizing the overarching teal architecture.

## Typography

Typography establishes structured hierarchy, combining geometric structural titles with open, highly legible body forms across screen sizes.

### Script Pairing & Dual-Engine Fallback
- **Latin Display & Titles:** `Manrope` delivers modern architectural punch, geometric forms, and high-density legibility for code titles, product names, and pricing.
- **Latin Body & Interactive Elements:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides tall x-heights, humanist clarity, and accessible reading across technical document descriptions and checkout interfaces.
- **Bengali Dual Integration:** For localization in Bengali (বাংলা), the design system cascades to `Hind Siliguri` alongside system fallbacks. Line-height tokens implicitly expand by a factor of `1.15` when Bengali locales are active to prevent matra vowel clip in ascenders and descenders.
- **Currency Typography:** The currency symbol `৳` (BDT) pairs with `Manrope` numbers using `currency-display` or `title-lg`, matching the numeric weight to prevent baseline misalignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes an adaptive 12-column fluid grid tailored to digital product density, cataloging, and dashboard views.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid, max canvas container `1320px`, `gutter` at `1.5rem` (24px), outer `margin` at `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `gutter` at `1.25rem` (20px), outer `margin-tablet` at `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4-column grid, `gutter-mobile` at `1rem` (16px), outer `margin-mobile` at `1rem` (16px).

### Spacing Cadence
Component internals follow an 8pt architectural rhythm with sub-step 4pt options:
- `space-xs` (4px) / `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-text spacing, badge padding, and metadata tag groupings.
- `space-md` (16px): Card internal padding, form row spacing, compact list separators.
- `space-lg` (24px): Standard card container padding, modular section groupings.
- `space-xl` (32px) / `space-2xl` (48px) / `space-3xl` (64px): Hero block clearances and major marketplace category segment separations.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, utilizing crisp micro-strokes layered with diffused, teal-tinted ambient illumination to convey physical quality.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base / Canvas):** Surface color `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation. Intended for main viewport background, sticky background navigation anchors, and footer zones.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Surfaces & Tiles):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface resting on a crisp border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Used for software spec sheets, asset cards in rest state, and filter accordions.
- **Level 2 (Active & Hover Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surface with light teal ambient lift. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Card border transitions to `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Used for focused asset cards, interactive checkout panels, and category pills.
- **Level 3 (Floating Overlays):** Dropdowns, multi-select search drawers, verified seller popovers. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Payment Sheets):** Instant purchase dialogs, MFS transfer confirmations, and full-screen drawer overlays. Shadow: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18)`. Backdrop blur: `blur(8px)` with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` overlay.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Level 2 (Rounded)** architecture to evoke approachable modern software tooling without losing structural discipline.

### Radius Assignments
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Primary form controls, inputs, standard buttons, badge pills, search bars, and technical tags.
- **Containers & Product Cards (`1rem` / 16px - `rounded-lg`):** Digital asset preview cards, license specification cards, modal windows, and pricing tier cards.
- **Feature Displays & Modals (`1.5rem` / 24px - `rounded-xl`):** Hero showcase panels, download confirmation dialogue cards, and checkout side-sheets.
- **Micro Radii (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Code snippet blocks, keyboard shortcut tags, and micro status pills.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Instant Purchase / Download):** Background `#0F766E`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.5rem`, font `label-lg`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover state: `#115E59` with subtle ambient glow. Active state: slight downward scale (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Action (Live Preview / Documentation):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F766E`, border `1px solid #0F766E`, border-radius `0.5rem`. Hover: `#F0FDFA`.
- **Ghost Action (Filter reset, dismiss):** Transparent background, text `#64748B`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F172A`.

### Badges & Verification Elements
- **Verified Seller Badge:** Pill shape, background `#CCFBF1`, text `#0F766E`, border `1px solid #99F6E4`. Contains checkmark icon leading text `Verified Seller` (বা `ভেরিফাইড সেলার`).
- **Currency & Price Component:** Features bold `Manrope` numerals with a prefixed `৳` symbol. Discounted pricing shows original crossed-out value in `#94A3B8` adjacent to the current price in `#0F766E` or `#0F172A`.
- **Payment Method Badges (bKash, Nagad, Cards):** High-clarity micro-capsules (`rounded-md`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `0.25rem 0.5rem`) showcasing vendor marks with crisp white background surfaces to instill security during checkout.

### Digital Product Cards
- **Structure:** Elevated white card (`rounded-lg`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, elevation Level 1).
- **Header:** 16:10 aspect ratio preview thumbnail with top-right overlay pill for framework tag (e.g., "Laravel", "React Native").
- **Body:** Product title in `title-lg`, seller line with miniature avatar and verified teal check, framework capability icons.
- **Footer:** Bottom split layout with currency indicator (`৳ 2,450`) on the left and quick-purchase/cart icon action on the right.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs & Search Fields:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, border-radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Typography `body-md`. Focus state: border `#0F766E`, focus ring `3px solid rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Size `20px x 20px`. Unchecked: border `1.5px solid #94A3B8`, background `#FFFFFF`. Checked: background `#0F766E`, border `#0F766E`, displaying a crisp white glyph.

### Navigation & Tab Bars
- **Desktop Navigation:** Elevated sticky top bar (`#FFFFFF` with `0.85` alpha and blur `12px`), bottom border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, category dropdown items, and localized language switcher (EN / বাং).
- **Mobile Bottom App Bar:** Fixed baseline dock (`height: 64px`) with items for Browse, Categories, Saved, and Account, using active indicators colored in `#0F766E`.
